📌 Project Overview

The ESG Research & Insight Hub is a design-forward, AI-enabled platform developed to bridge the "Interpretation Gap" in corporate sustainability reporting. While thousands of companies now publish ESG disclosures, these reports are often 200+ pages long, fragmented, and difficult for non-experts to analyze.



This project uses Agentic AI to synthesize complex disclosures into structured, actionable insights. Rather than just extracting data, the tool applies a custom "Consultant-Logic" framework to evaluate the significance of the findings, helping users identify transparency gaps and potential greenwashing.



🔍 The Problem: The "Interpretation Gap"

  • Information Overload: Sustainability reports are too long for quick manual review, leading to critical data being missed.


  • Data Fragmentation: Relevant ESG news and controversies are often disconnected from the company's official claims.

  • The "What Now?" Factor: Most tools show raw numbers but fail to explain what those numbers mean in the context of global standards (like CSRD or GRI) or industry benchmarks.

🛠️ The Technical Architecture (No-Code Stack)

This project demonstrates a high level of technical literacy by "chaining" professional AI tools into a seamless workflow:

  • The Brain (Agentic AI): Built using Flowise, orchestrating Gemini 3 Flash to act as an autonomous researcher that can plan tasks, search the web, and audit PDFs.

  • The Model: Utilizes the Gemini 3 series for its industry-leading context window (1M+ tokens), allowing the AI to "read" entire 200-page reports without losing data continuity.

  • The Library (Database): Airtable serves as the central repository, storing structured metrics and summaries for 50–100 initial firms to ensure high speed and data integrity.

  • The Face (Interface): An interactive AI Research Assistant widget embedded directly into a personal GitHub Pages website, allowing for real-time user interaction.

🚀 Methodology: The 2-Month Roadmap

The project is built in two distinct phases to ensure the AI's logic is accurate and grounded in sustainability expertise.

Phase 1: Human-in-the-Loop Curation (Month 1)

  • Manual audit of the "Top 50" firms using Google AI Studio.

  • Developing the "Consultant-Logic" prompts to identify specific red flags (e.g., missing Scope 3 emissions or vague Net Zero targets).

  • Establishing the baseline data library in Airtable.

Phase 2: Agentic Automation (Month 2)

  • Translating manual audit logic into Flowise workflows.

  • Enabling "Live Research" capabilities where the AI can audit firms outside the initial database on demand.

  • Final deployment and embedding of the tool onto the GitHub-hosted portfolio.

📈 Key Outcomes

  • Efficiency: Reduces the time required for initial company ESG screening by approximately 80%.

  • Clarity: Translates qualitative "fluff" into quantitative metrics and executive-level summaries.

  • Scalability: Creates a repeatable framework that can be expanded to include thousands of firms across different sectors.
